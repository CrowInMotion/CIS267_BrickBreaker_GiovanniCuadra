# CIS267\_BrickBreaker\_GiovanniCuadra

vampBreaker



Classic break breaker with a vampire twist.



The only thing standing between you and a good meal is a few villagers, good luck bloodsucker.



The vampiric paddle is controlled with 'W' and 'D' or <- and ->, bounce the magic ball at the villagers until they're all dead, and keep it from going out of bounds.



A group of villagers are worth 100 points and die in two hits.

Sheep are worth 50 points and die in one hit.

Tough villagers are worth 150, die in three hits, and give an extra life.

Mirrors are worth 150, die in two hits, and spawn a duplicate ball.



Blood packs fall from the sky and provide a movement speed boost to the player.

Coffins fall from the sky and provide a movement speed boost to the magic ball.

Crosses fall from the sky and damage the player.



If you lose all lives you get a game over screen.

If you destroy all blocks, you also get the game over screen.
